Privacy Policy – AdsApp Rewards

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what data the AdsApp Rewards Android app ("the App") collects, why, and what choices you have. The App is operated by Manvel Ghazaryan, Armenia. Contact: ghazaryanmanvel999@gmail.com.

1. Data stored on your device

Your coin balance, the number of ads watched per day and your transaction history are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to the cloud. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Data you give us when you request a withdrawal

When you request a payout, the App opens your e-mail app with a pre-filled message containing a request ID, the amount, the payout method and the e-mail address you entered. We use this information only to verify the request and send the payout, and we keep it for up to 24 months for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. To have it deleted earlier, write to ghazaryanmanvel999@gmail.com.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process the following data: the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and interactions with ads. This data is used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where you have consented, personalised advertising. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

3a. Offers (AdGem)

The "Offers" section is provided by AdGem. When you open it, AdGem receives a random player ID generated by the App (not your name or e-mail), your device and app information, IP address and advertising ID, and it tracks which offers you complete in order to credit your reward and prevent fraud. Offers are run by third-party advertisers under their own privacy policies. See AdGem's privacy policy.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

We process withdrawal data to perform our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and to prevent fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)). Personalised advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

5. Children

The App is intended only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact ghazaryanmanvel999@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Security

Data sent by Google's SDKs is encrypted in transit. Withdrawal e-mails are handled by your own e-mail provider and ours.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
